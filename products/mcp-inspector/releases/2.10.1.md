# mcp-inspector 2.10.1

## Why The Patch?
The addition of the new `mcpdo` client threw a curveball into the default `npx @modelcontextprotocol/inspector@latest` method of running the inspector, because there were now two binaries to run, the `launcher` (used by the other clients) and `mcpdo`. This quick follow-up patch restores sanity so that command works again. Instructions for launching new `mcpdo` client are on `README.md`.
