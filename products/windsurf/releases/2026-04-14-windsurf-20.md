<h2 class="body2 mb-4 mt-0 font-medium text-sk-black" id="devin-in-windsurf"><a class="text-sk-sea hover:underline" href="#devin-in-windsurf" aria-hidden="true" tabindex="-1"><span class="icon icon-link"></span></a>Devin in Windsurf</h2>
<ul class="body3 mb-10 ml-4 mt-0 list-disc space-y-2 text-sk-black"><li class="body3 text-sk-black">Devin cloud agent available directly inside Windsurf, included with every self-serve plan</li><li class="body3 text-sk-black">Delegate tasks from a local session to Devin with one click; Devin runs on its own VM</li><li class="body3 text-sk-black">Review Devin changes and test results without leaving the editor</li><li class="body3 text-sk-black">Billing is based on your existing quota and extra usage, with up to 50 USD in extra usage added for launching your first Devin Cloud session.</li></ul>
<p class="body3 mb-10 mt-0 text-sk-black"><strong class="font-medium text-sk-black">Note:</strong> Access to Devin Cloud is rolling out gradually. If you don't see it yet, try logging out of the website and IDE then logging back in.</p>
<p class="body3 mb-10 mt-0 text-sk-black">Devin Cloud is disabled by default for enterprise accounts. Enterprise admins should enable Devin access in their organization settings if they have already purchased Cognition Platform.</p>
