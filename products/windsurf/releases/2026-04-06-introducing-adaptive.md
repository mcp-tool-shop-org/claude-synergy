<span data-as="p">A new <strong>Adaptive</strong> model option is now available in the model picker. Adaptive intelligently selects the best model for each task, helping you make your quota last longer by avoiding overuse of premium models.</span>
<ul>
<li><strong>Availability</strong>: Now available to all self-serve users on Pro, Max, and Teams plans.</li>
<li><strong>Dynamic model selection</strong> - Automatically chooses the right underlying model for your task while drawing down quota at a fixed per-token rate.</li>
<li><strong>Extra usage promo</strong> - Beyond your quota, extra usage is offered at 0.50 USD per 1M input tokens, 2.00 USD per 1M output tokens, and 0.10 USD per 1M cache read tokens for the next 2 weeks.</li>
</ul>
