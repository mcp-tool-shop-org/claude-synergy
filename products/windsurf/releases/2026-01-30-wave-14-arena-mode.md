<span data-as="p">Run two Cascade agents side-by-side with hidden model identities and vote on which performs better. Arena Mode lets you discover which models actually work best for <em>your</em> workflow, codebase, and tasks—not just what benchmarks or influencers say.</span>
<ul>
<li><strong>Battle Groups</strong>: Choose specific models to compare or let Windsurf randomly select from curated groups like “fast models” vs “smart models”</li>
<li><strong>Personal &amp; Global Leaderboards</strong>: Your votes contribute to both a personal leaderboard (your preferences) and a global one (across all Windsurf users)</li>
<li><strong>Sync or Branch</strong>: Send followup prompts to both agents simultaneously, or branch and explore different paths individually</li>
</ul>
<span data-as="p">To get started, select the new <strong>Arena</strong> tab in the model picker. All battle groups are free for the first week for paid users.</span>
