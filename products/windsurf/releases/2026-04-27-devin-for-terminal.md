# windsurf — Devin for Terminal

<p class="body3 mb-10 mt-0 text-sk-black">Devin is now available <a class="text-sk-sea hover:underline" href="https://devin.ai/terminal">for Terminal</a>. All Windsurf users can use this new CLI agent with your existing subscription.</p>
<ul class="body3 mb-10 ml-4 mt-0 list-disc space-y-2 text-sk-black"><li class="body3 text-sk-black"><strong class="font-medium text-sk-black">Runs on your machine</strong> — Optimized for interactive work, with full access to your codebase, tools, and environment.</li><li class="body3 text-sk-black"><strong class="font-medium text-sk-black">Hand off to the cloud</strong> — Seamless hand off to Devin in the cloud, with its own VM, testing, video recordings, autofix and more. Come back to a finished PR.</li><li class="body3 text-sk-black"><strong class="font-medium text-sk-black">Multi-model</strong> — All of your favorite frontier models in one place, including Opus 4.7, GPT-5.5, and SWE-1.6.</li><li class="body3 text-sk-black"><strong class="font-medium text-sk-black">Fast</strong> — Written in Rust and so performant that the binary can run on an original VT100.</li></ul>
<h2 class="body2 mb-4 mt-0 font-medium text-sk-black" id="devin-agent-in-windsurf"><a class="text-sk-sea hover:underline" href="#devin-agent-in-windsurf" aria-hidden="true" tabindex="-1"><span class="icon icon-link"></span></a>Devin Agent in Windsurf</h2>
<p class="body3 mb-10 mt-0 text-sk-black">You can also enable the new Devin Local agent in Windsurf. This is the same agent harness used on the terminal and sessions can be accessed from both Windsurf and the CLI.</p>
<p class="body3 mb-10 mt-0 text-sk-black">In our testing, it's up to 30% more token-efficient than the existing Cascade agent.</p>
