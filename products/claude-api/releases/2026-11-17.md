# claude-api — November 17, 2026

<ul class="docs-public-part">
<li id="linked-billing">We’ve launched <a href="/docs/en/manage-claude/oauth-apps/linked-billing">Linked billing</a> in beta with the <code>user-profiles-billing-2026-10-01</code> beta header. Your customers can now pay for the Claude usage in your app through their own Claude organization. Once a customer links their organization, set <code>billing_policy</code> on each <code>/v1/messages</code> or <code>/v1/chat/completions</code> request to choose who pays. In the Claude Console, customers can set a spend cap for your app or unlink your app. Linked billing works for customers whose organization pays Anthropic directly for API usage.</li>
</ul>
