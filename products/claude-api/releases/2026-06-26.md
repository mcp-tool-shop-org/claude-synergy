# claude-api — June 26, 2026

<ul>
<li>We’ve raised <a href="/docs/en/api/rate-limits">rate limits</a> across the Claude API. Claude Sonnet and Claude Haiku rate limits now match Claude Opus at every usage tier, and usage tiers have been consolidated into three: Start, Build, and Scale. Most organizations move to a higher tier, no organization receives lower limits than before, and no action is required. You can <a href="/settings/limits">view your tier and current limits</a> in the Claude Console.</li>
</ul>
