# cursor — Long-running Agents in Research Preview

<p>Cursor can now work autonomously over longer horizons to complete larger, more complex tasks. Long-running agents plan first and finish more difficult work without human intervention.</p>
<p>In research preview and internal testing, long-running agents completed work that was previously too hard for regular agents. This led to larger, more complete PRs with fewer obvious follow-ups.</p>
