# cursor — Customize Cursor

<p>Plugins, skills, and MCPs let you customize Cursor for your workflows. The new Customize page brings them into one place.</p>
<p>You can now add and manage plugins, skills, MCPs, subagents, rules, commands, and hooks at the user, team, or workspace level, and even bring your own custom MCPs.</p>
<h2>Marketplace leaderboard</h2>
<p>Cursor now shows you a leaderboard of the most popular plugins, skills, and MCPs across your team.</p>
<p>Add any to your setup with one click from the new Customize page and extend Cursor for your workflow.</p>
<h2>Plugin canvases</h2>
<p>Plugins now support prebuilt canvases: shared setup templates your team can open and reuse.</p>
<p>Use the Hex Canvas to build data visualizations.</p>
<p>Use the Atlassian Canvas to see a realtime view of all our issues, projects, and documents.</p>
<h2>New Team Marketplaces</h2>
<p><a href="https://cursor.com/docs/plugins#team-marketplaces">Team marketplaces</a> now support imports of plugin repositories from GitLab, BitBucket, or Azure DevOps so you easily add plugins and distribute them to your team.</p>
<p>Learn more in our <a href="https://cursor.com/docs/customize-cursor">docs</a>.</p>
