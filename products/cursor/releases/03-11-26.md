# cursor — New Plugins on the Cursor Marketplace

<p>We&#39;ve added more than 30 new <a href="https://cursor.com/docs/plugins">plugins</a> from partners such as Atlassian, Datadog, GitLab, Glean, Hugging Face, monday.com, and PlanetScale. Cursor can now read from, write to, and take actions across more of your stack.</p>

<p>Try out the new plugins at <a href="https://cursor.com/marketplace">cursor.com/marketplace</a>, or read more in our <a href="https://cursor.com/blog/new-plugins">announcement</a>.</p>
