# Control Cowork from your phone with a persistent thread (research preview for Pro/Max) — March 17, 2026

Users on Pro and Max plans can access a persistent agent thread via Claude Desktop or Claude for iOS/Android to manage tasks in Cowork. We're rolling this out to Max plans starting today, and Pro plans over the next two days. For more information, see Assign tasks to Claude from anywhere in Cowork.
