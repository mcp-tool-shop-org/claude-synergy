# anthropic-apps — November 20, 2026

<ul><li><strong>A refreshed look for Claude Mobile:</strong> A refreshed look for Claude Mobile is rolling out gradually, starting with Claude for iOS. Your chats, projects, and plan stay the same. There's nothing to turn on. Keep your app updated, and note that the new look needs iOS 26 or later.</li><li><strong>Computer use (beta) for Team and Enterprise plans:</strong> Computer use lets Claude see a user's screen and work in the desktop apps they allow. It works in the new Claude experience in Claude Desktop, and from Claude on the web and Claude Mobile where <b>Computer access</b> is on. It's now available in beta on Team and Enterprise plans, and is rolling out to organizations gradually. For more information, see <b><a href="https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18483812-set-up-computer-use-for-team-and-enterprise-plans">Set up computer use for Team and Enterprise plans</a></b>.</li></ul>
